A NOTE FROM THE MAKER / CV SUMMARY

SHURUIREN

I've always been exploring the spaces between Design, Illustration, and Digital Media.

Studying Interdisciplinary Design taught me to start with problems and look across disciplines for answers. Later, during my master's in Digital Media, I began bringing the storytelling of illustration and the warmth of handmade work into screens, so interfaces and motion could have emotion and room to breathe.

To me, creativity is the sum of observation and connection: noticing details others might miss and bringing seemingly unrelated things together into something that feels naturally whole.

I love poetic imagery, considered white space, and motion with intention. I keep experimenting with tools and media, exploring their unique qualities and technological possibilities. Ultimately, I care most about how the work makes people feel—technology makes it possible, and sincerity makes it resonate.
